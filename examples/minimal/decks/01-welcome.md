# Three ways to present

- **One computer and a projector:** press **S** for the speaker view, with notes and a timer.
- **Two devices:** start a session; the projector follows your laptop or phone.
- **No server of your own:** synchronize through a public relay.

::: notes
These notes appear in the speaker view and on the phone controller. They never reach the projection: the server removes them, and the audience copy built for relays has none.
:::

# On a phone

Open the control link on your phone: the slide is at the top, your notes in the middle, **Back / Next** and the timer at the bottom.

Tap the timer when you actually start, to reset it to 0:00.

::: notes
Under the timer: "on time", "slow down" or "speed up", from the pacing markers in these notes.
:::
